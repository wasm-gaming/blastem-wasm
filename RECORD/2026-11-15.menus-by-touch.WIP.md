# The app's menus by touch

2026-11-15 13:34 · owner: "Sigamos hasta dejar los mandos bien pulidos". The same change as fbneo-wasm's app
(`RECORD/2026-11-15.gamepad-as-player-one.WIP.md`, "the app's menus by touch"), which is this app on
FBNeo's SDK — verified there end to end on a phone.

**Status:** being implemented.

## Why

On a phone the launcher draws engine-specs' touch pad, which hides while a menu is open: the menus
are tapped. The pause menu works by touch, but the suspend-point screen has actions only a gamepad
button reaches — B (back / exit / cancel), Y (save the unsaved point into a slot; a saved one's
options) — a tap on a card plays it, the options sheet has no way back but B, and the hints show
gamepad glyphs.

## Plan

1. Hints with an action are buttons (`data-act`): a tap runs the same handler as the gamepad
   (`onAction`): A → accept, B → back, Y → more. The sheet gains "B volver".
2. A long press on a card (500 ms, not a mouse) focuses it and runs `more`; the click after the lift
   is dropped; no context menu on the overlay.
3. On `pointer: coarse`: 44 px hint pills without glyphs; gamepad-only hints hidden.
