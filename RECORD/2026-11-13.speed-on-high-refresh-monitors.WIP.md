## Report

## What BlastEm does (pinned rev 6d1a7447ee9e)
- `sync_source video` → `emscripten_set_main_loop(browser_main_loop, 0, 0)`: one emulated frame
  per `requestAnimationFrame`, and `display_hz = 60; //TODO: FIXME` under emscripten. A 120 Hz
  monitor would run it at exactly 2×.
- `sync_source audio` (upstream `default.cfg` default, ours) → `setTimeout` timing of
  `samples * 500 / freq` ms, and the main loop skips while the audio buffers are full: paced by
  audio, not by the monitor. No fallback to video if audio fails (`fatal_error`).

## Measured (published 0.1.1, Chromium, a ROM counting VBlanks into SRAM)
~60 emulated frames/s in every case: rAF capped (~61/s) and uncapped (~86/s, headless can't
reach 120), AudioContext running and forced suspended, after pause/resume, restart and state
save + load. Not reproduced.

## How the other engines pace (for when it comes back)
- fbneo: C patch, `emscripten_get_now()` deadline at the driver's `nBurnFPS`.
- rsdkv4: C patch of `RetroEngine::Run`, logic stepped by SDL's performance counter.
- snes9x / mGBA / geolith: JS loop on rAF runs as many frames as the audio buffer needs; with
  audio blocked, a wall-clock accumulator at the core frame rate, capped per tick.
